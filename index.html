About

Designing since 2018

I am a brand identity and publication designer based between Lagos and Bayelsa, Nigeria. My work covers visual identity systems, book covers and typesetting, editorial design, packaging and campaigns, and I take each project from brief to print-ready files.

I have designed 19 covers for The Roaring Lion in Newcastle, the first GoLearn magazine, the GoRealty and Homes identity, and live quote graphics for RiSE Summit 2026. I also head the Graphic Design Unit in the Department of Fine and Applied Arts at Niger Delta University, where I teach and mentor students.

MA, Graphic Design · Niger Delta University, 2023
BA, Fine and Applied Arts (First Class) · Niger Delta University, 2017
