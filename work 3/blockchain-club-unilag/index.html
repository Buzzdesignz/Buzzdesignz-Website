BlockChain Club Unilag

Brand system for a university blockchain club

BlockChain Club Unilag is a student blockchain club at the University of Lagos. Another designer created the logo mark. I took the project over and developed it into a full system: the lockups and colourways, typography, colour palette, patterns, mock-ups and the brand manual.
