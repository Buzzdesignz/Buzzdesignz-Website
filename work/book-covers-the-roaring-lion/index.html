Book Covers

19 covers for The Roaring Lion, Newcastle

Between 2020 and 2023 I designed 19 book covers for The Roaring Lion, an independent publisher in Newcastle, UK — mostly poetry collections, plus non-fiction titles. Each cover was delivered as a print-ready full wrap: front, spine and back. Working remotely with the publisher and authors, I aimed to give every book a single clear idea that reads at thumbnail size and still rewards a closer look.
