RiSE Summit 2026

145+ live quote graphics for a one-day summit

Patricks RiSE Summit 2026 — From Cradle to Contribution — brought together parents, educators, advocates and self-advocates around autism, disability and inclusion in Lagos. I joined the design team close to the event and worked within the summit's established identity. Working remotely, I built a quote template and turned each speaker's words into a branded graphic as their session ended, ready to publish before the next speakers began — 145+ graphics across the day.
