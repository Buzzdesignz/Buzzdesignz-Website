Brand identity · 2024

GoRealty & Homes

Brand identity for a new real estate start-up

GoRealty and Homes is a new real estate brand within Go Holdings, dealing in lands, homes and offices. As a start-up with no formal brief, it needed an identity that would feel trustworthy and established from day one — and personal, built around caring for clients rather than just making sales. I designed the identity from scratch: logo, colour, typography, pattern, stationery and merchandise.
