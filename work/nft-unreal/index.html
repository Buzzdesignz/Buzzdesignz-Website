Brand identity · 2020

NFT Unreal

Brand identity for an NFT explainer brand

NFT Unreal is a brand about explaining NFTs. For its identity, interlocking bands fold into an N beneath a separate roof piece, set beside a bold NFT and a spaced-out UNREAL. I built the mark in solid and outline versions, three brand colours with colourways for each, and mock-ups across merchandise, print and signage.
