Enugu Bird Club

Brand identity for a bird club

Enugu Bird Club is a bird club in Enugu, Nigeria. I drew a bird in flight from one bold navy line, filled four of its panels with bright colour, and built a five-colour palette and colourways around it, shown here on T-shirt mock-ups.
