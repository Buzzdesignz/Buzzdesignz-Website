Sister Warriors Conquest

One brief, six interpretations, one design

An event flyer for the Daughters of Zion fellowship of MFM International HQ Youth Church: a prayer session themed “Go in this thy might” (Judges 6:14). The brief was fixed: theme, date, venue and two prayer captains. Before settling on a final, I explored the same content six ways, across three visual directions.

Client
MFM Int’l HQ Youth Church, Daughters of Zion
Event
27 March 2022, Yaba, Lagos
Scope
Square event flyer, 6 concepts
Tools
