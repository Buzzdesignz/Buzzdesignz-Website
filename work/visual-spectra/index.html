Publication · 2025

Visual Spectra

Exhibition catalogue for a university convocation show

Visual Spectra: A Convocation Exhibition of Form-Generation in NDU showed work by staff and students of the Department of Fine and Applied Arts, Niger Delta University, as part of the university’s 7th combined convocation. I designed the 19-page catalogue: cover, section system and every layout, from the foreword to the plates of painting, sculpture, textiles, ceramics, graphic design and metal design. I was also an exhibitor: my Scent Leaf Herbal Tea banner hangs in the graphic design section.

Client
Dept. of Fine and Applied Arts, NDU
Exhibition
