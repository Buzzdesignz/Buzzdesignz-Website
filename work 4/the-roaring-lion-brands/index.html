Brand identity · 2021–2022

The Roaring Lion

A brand family for a Newcastle publisher

For The Roaring Lion Newcastle Ltd, an independent publisher, I created three identities from scratch to the client's brief: the master brand for the publishing house, The Newcastle Review for its editorial content, and Lion & Lilac for its foundation and events. Each mark was traced from a photograph and refined into a clean vector, so the three brands feel distinct but clearly related.
